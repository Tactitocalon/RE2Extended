Shield Reserve Cell
 - CV (maybe SV in the future)
 - Can be manually used like a "gun" to instantly increase the shield amount (but not over the maximum).
 - Each time it's clicked, a Shield Reserve Cell is used (use the "power off" model for a depleted cell). Won't restore
 shields unless the cell will restore its full shield amount (so if your shields are near full or full, it won't activate).
 - Each Shield Reserve Cell instantly recharges 8000 shield (but not over the maximum shield amount).
 - Explosive, like generators.
 - Use "PowerRegulator" model.
 - 500W power consumption, 1000 CPU cost.
 - Requires 15 minutes at full shields to recharge. Check every 30 seconds to see if shields are full. If interrupted, the
 timer resets.

Constructor Manager
 - Use ConsoleDesk_C05
 - Has a new UI - the mass constructor UI. This should be based off the normal Constructor UI.
 - Right side: templates, just like the normal Constructor UI.
 - Middle: construction queue, just like the normal Constructor UI. It won't function the same way, though. We will call this the "Job Queue".
   - The Job Queue UI will ONLY have the input section, NOT the output section.
   - The output section will have some controls: "Start Job", "Cancel Job".
 - Left side: the new "job manager" UI.
 - Job Manager UI:
   - List of "jobs". In the Job List, each job has a "Name", "Type", and "Completed".
   - For now, Type will be a stub. For Type, just put "Once".
   - At the bottom of the Job Manager UI will be a button for "New Job".
 - When the user creates a "New Job", it creates a new row in the Job Manager UI.
 - Selecting a Job will show all the items queued for that job in the Job Queue. A newly created job will be treated as "Pending"
 - Clicking an item in the Templates section will queue that item in the Job Queue.
 - Clicking "Start Job" will distribute all items in the Job Queue to all constructors on the ship. Items are distributed evenly, with consideration
   to which constructors can actually process which items. For example, if an item requires a Universal Constructor, that item will be evenly distributed
   to all Universal Constructors.
 - Once a job is started, the Completed status moves to "Started", and the Start Job button is disabled for that job.
 - The "Cancel Job" button will remove that job from the job manager. In the future, we will also use it to cancel the queued items from all construction managers.

Fuel Distribution Regulator
 - CV / BA
 - Increases the amount of power generated by generators, based on the number of fuel tanks you have.
 - Also increases the effectiveness of plasma turbines, based on the number of fuel tanks you have.
 - It's designed to give you an incentive to have lots of fuel tanks.
 - 0.25% / 0.5% / 1% increased power for small / medium / large fuel tanks, up to a maximum of 25%.
 - Same ratio for plasma turbine effectiveness, up to a maximum of 25%.
 - This is implemented by the Fuel Distribution Regulator changing its own power out / consumption reduction based on
 what other devices exist on the vessel, rather than mutating the generators / plasma turbines directly.
 - Use LandClaimDevice for the model.
 - Very explosive, like generators.
 - Limit 2. 10000 CPU cost. (so maximum performance is 25 fuel tanks + 2 fuel distribution regulators)

FACTORY MANAGEMENT

Factories are BA-only machines that can perform bulk operations over a long period of time.
They might allow for higher efficiencies than constructors or smelters (eg. performing smelts with higher yields than the
regular recipes).
They need to be able to operate while not loaded (aka, we should calculate how long it's been until it was loaded, and
apply the operations retroactively).

Various factories might require different environmental conditions:
 - Some processes might require zero, normal or high gravity.
 - Mining machines are also a possibility (use the mining deco blocks?) to provide passive income, depending on planet type.
 - They might also just be required for certain processes (eg. "providing" a non-item resource to allow a certain factory process)

Factory items might not be handled like regular Empyrion items. No way to directly withdraw/retrieve them? IDK.

In the future, maybe have ways for Zirax / Pirates to attack bases with certain high value factories?
